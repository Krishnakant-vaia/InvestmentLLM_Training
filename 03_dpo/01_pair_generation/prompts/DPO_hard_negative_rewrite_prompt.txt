WHAT THE NEW ANSWER MUST DO

1. SAME MISTAKE. Reach the same wrong final result by the same wrong method or the same wrong reading as the original answer. Do not invent a different mistake and do not make it more wrong than the original.
2. SAME FORM AS THE PREFERRED ANSWER. If the preferred answer shows a formula, show the formula; if it shows several steps, show several steps.
3. THE MISTAKE IS CARRIED THROUGH. Every number after the mistake must follow correctly from it, so the working is internally consistent. Only the mistaken step is wrong.
4. NO ADMISSION AND NO COMMENTARY. The answer writes with the same confidence as the preferred one. It never says or hints that something is wrong: do not use words such as mistakenly, wrongly, incorrectly, error, misread, misapplied, inverted, ignored, omitted, skipped, forgot, instead of, rather than, should have. Do not add bracketed remarks about the method, notes after the answer, hedges, or phrases such as "in other words".
5. NO COPYING THE CORRECT ANSWER. Do not use the preferred answer's final result. Keep wording, units, and number formats the same as the preferred answer's.

Never describe the mistake as an operation or as a deliberate choice (for example "I shifted each item one place", "I added one to the count", "I swapped the numerator and denominator", "I used X in place of Y"). The reasoning must contain nothing that tells a reader the conclusion is wrong.
8. USE ONLY WHAT THE QUESTION GIVES. Do not add facts that are not in the question.
