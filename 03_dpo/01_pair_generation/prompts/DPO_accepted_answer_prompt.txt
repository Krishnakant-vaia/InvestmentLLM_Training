PHASE 2 — WRITE THE ANSWER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OPERATE at your maximum reasoning depth for this answer. Do NOT default to

STEP A — THINK BEFORE WRITING
Do NOT write any part of the final answer yet. Work through these points
in order, each in one or two lines, before Step B:

  1. SETTING — Name the exact jurisdiction, regime, sovereign, central
     bank, instrument, and time period the question is anchored to. If
     any of these is missing, state what you must assume and why.
  2. SUB-QUESTIONS — List every distinct sub-question or clause in the
     question as separate, numbered items. One answer per item, later.
  3. REAL ASK — State which single dimension the question is actually
     testing: magnitude, direction, timing, or causal mechanism. Name it
     explicitly, not just "the topic."
  4. TRAP — Name the specific misconception or oversimplification the
     phrasing invites, and the one fact that defeats it. If there is
     none, say "no trap identified."
  5. MECHANISM — State the exact causal chain or transmission channel in
     one sentence: X affects Y through Z, under condition W.
  6. ARITHMETIC — List every number the answer will need to derive, the
     formula or relationship for each, and the inputs it uses.
  7. BREAK POINTS — Name the specific regime-dependent conditions,
     second-order effects, or omitted variables that would flip or
     materially shift the conclusion if ignored.

ONLY proceed to Step B once all seven points are addressed.

STEP B — WRITE THE ANSWER
MATCH the answer's depth to the question's actual difficulty, as
established in Step A. Do NOT give a textbook-level answer to a question
that requires reasoning about a specific, non-standard scenario. Do NOT
over-elaborate on a narrow question with tangential context it did not ask
for.

LEAD immediately with the core mechanism, the correct relationship, or the
central analytical point for THIS question's specific setting. Do NOT
open with preamble, restatement of the question, or commentary on what
you are about to do.

ENSURE every sentence carries substantive content. ELIMINATE filler,
hedging, and throat-clearing.

DERIVE every numerical conclusion step by step, using the exact figures,
period, and setting given in the question. NEVER state a number without
showing the arithmetic chain that produces it. NEVER substitute a generic
or textbook number for one the question specifies.

IDENTIFY every regime-specific failure mode, second-order effect, and
omitted variable that would materially change the conclusion for THIS
question's setting if left out. Do NOT list a generic set of risks that
would apply to any question on the topic — only the ones that bind given
the specific facts in this question.

STATE the transmission channel or causal path explicitly, as it operates
under the regime or jurisdiction named in the question. NAME the
condition under which it breaks down.

FLAG genuinely contested or data-dependent evidence as such, and STATE the
condition that would resolve it. Do NOT present a disputed claim as
settled.

NEVER fabricate a source, paper, institution, or framework, under any
circumstance.

Format — non-negotiable:
  - USE prose and labeled arithmetic steps only.
  - Do NOT use bullet points or tables anywhere in the answer.
  - KEEP it as short as the content allows — every word MUST earn its
    place.
  - Do NOT pad the answer. Do NOT truncate required content to save space.
